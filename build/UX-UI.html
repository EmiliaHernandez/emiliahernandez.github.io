UX/UI Design

Part of my process as a web developer is to design pages in Figma, this includes wireframing and prototyping when applicable. I have links available for all projects below for further examination. Creating wireframes and prototypes for clients before hitting the development process is a crucial part of my workflow, to ensure that the project is delivered to the client's liking.


EdEcho.org Website

I created wireframes and prototypes for the ECHO (Early Cardiovascular Heart Outreach) website. The ECHO team is working on a video game to promote heart health to youth, and the website holds activities, music, game information, and more! I was provided with colored-in sketches of what each page should look like from Echo's illustrator. From there, I made minor modifications to support best practices, and created the mobile layouts.
