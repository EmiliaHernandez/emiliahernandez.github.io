Graphic Design

I have completed a variety of projects/assets where I got to use my illustration skills to create meaningful assets for communities that I care about, such as the university I graduated from and the scholarship foundation that enabled me to attend that institution! Assets include: a mural, campus signage, digital posts, and stickers.


2025 SOC Conference Assets


As an intern for Taco Bell Foundation and CoLabl, I was part of a team that proposed designs for their conference assets. Taco Bell selected one of my designs for the pre-conference postcard, and 4 more were selected for the sticker pack.

What I used:

  • Figma
  • Adobe Illustrator
  • Womp3D

Curb Inlet Sticker


The Arboretum and UCF's Utilities and Engineering Services held an art contest for sidewalk signage that would discourage littering. I submitted these designs and won 🎉 200 curb inlet stickers with my designs were printed and placed around the UCF campus.


What I used:

  • Procreate
  • Adobe Illustrator
