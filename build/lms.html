Live Mas Scholarship(LMS) Assets

In 2018, I was a recipient of Taco Bell Foundation's Live Mas Scholarship, and I have had experience doing design work for the foundation. This includes print and digital media that was designed for current scholars and alumni.


2025 Summer of Connection Conference Assets


As an intern for Taco Bell Foundation and CoLabl, I was part of a team that proposed designs for their conference assets. Taco Bell selected one of my designs for the pre-conference postcard, and 4 more were selected for the sticker pack. For my sticker designs, I combined elements provided in the brand guide with vectors that I created to make these vibrant stickers. For my postcard design, I combined 3D and 2D techniques to create the final look. The suitcases are 3D models with a toon shader, and I later converted the render into a vector so that I could add the stickers, text, and other supporting elements of the design in Illustrator.

What I used:

  • Figma
  • Adobe Illustrator
  • Womp3D
