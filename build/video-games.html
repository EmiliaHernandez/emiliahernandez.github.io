Game Development

Since 2018, I've been making games for weekend-long game jams. I have been the project/art/writing lead for all the ones below. Games are my favorite type of interactive media as a consumer, and I've enjoyed making them as an artist/dev who loves digital media and story-telling.


Lucky Little Darlings

created for LDJAM #55

LLD is a hidden object game with mostly narrative elements, focusing on a trio of siblings that have magical capabilities. They run an online shop of retro/vintage items that are cloned with magic, but it isn't bringing in enough money, so they decide to summon lovable creatures to make a business with them that will capture the adoration of the masses(because they are so cute and cute sells).


Created in 72 hours

Her Bedtime Stories

created for LDJAM #48

Sensitive Content Warning:

Her Bedtime Stories is a visual novel about a recovering victim of assault who is stuck in a loop of negative memories and interpretations. Her Bedtime Stories tells the story of giving in to thoughts related to trauma, through the visual device of a platform that is constantly sinking deeper into a mine. Your role is to observe and help when you can.

Spirit Scouts

created for LDJAM #47

Spirit scouts is a sentence puzzle game where you are looking for your soul mate in outer-space, based on surrounding bits of conversations. As you make a statement, find the corresponding response. Play as different recently deceased souls looking for someone they have been missing from their lifetime.
